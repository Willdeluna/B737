✅ B737 ATA 22 • Main Points Round 2

DFCS Made Line-Mechanic Simple

Hover over each idea. Think of ATA 22 as the command system: MCP/FMC inputs go to the FCC “brains,” then the airplane moves through actuators, trim, and autothrottle.

🎛️
Command Layer
MCP, FMC, and pilot inputs tell the airplane what mode, path, speed, heading, or altitude is desired.
🧠
Brain Layer
FCC A and FCC B process the logic for autopilot, flight director, autothrottle, and trim.
⚙️
Execution Layer
Actuators, trim systems, and autothrottle servos do the work after the logic is valid.
🧠

Big System Understanding — How It All Connects

DFCS = brain of autopilot, flight director, and autothrottle→Everything runs through the FCCs.big picture
Two FCCs, A and B, give full redundancy→If one fails, the other can still run the system.backup logic
MCP is the main control panel→Modes, speed, heading, altitude, and vertical commands come from here.
FMCS feeds DFCS with navigation data→LNAV and VNAV depend on FMC inputs.nav source
✈️

Autopilot — What Matters on the Line

Two modes: CMD and CWS→CMD is fully automatic. CWS is pilot assisted control.mode basics
Autopilot controls ailerons and elevators→It does not directly “grab” surfaces. It drives actuators.
You can override autopilot with force→Roughly 25–50 lb override prevents dangerous runaway conditions.safety clue
Both autopilots are needed for autoland→Single autopilot is normal flight. Dual autopilot is autoland logic.
🧭

Flight Director

F/D = visual guidance only→Magenta bars do not move anything. They tell the pilot where to go.
If F/D data is bad, the bars disappear→BOV is a big clue during troubleshooting.troubleshooting clue
F/D modes match autopilot modes→LNAV, VNAV, HDG SEL, LOC, and other modes line up with DFCS logic.
🚀

Autothrottle

A/T physically moves thrust levers→Servo motors move the levers. This is not just a display mode.
Two main jobs: SPD and N1→SPD holds speed. N1 holds thrust.core jobs
THR HOLD means no servo movement→Around 80 knots, it prevents unwanted throttle movement on takeoff.normal behavior
RETARD mode = idle thrust→Normal during descent or flare. Do not chase it like an automatic fault.
⚠️

Modes & Annunciations

FMA at the top of the PFD is system truth→Before troubleshooting behavior, verify what mode the airplane thinks it is in.check first
Green = active, white = armed, amber = caution→The color tells you what the mode is doing right now.
Mode changes show a green box for about 10 seconds→Use the box to verify the transition actually happened.
Wrong mode = wrong airplane behavior→Always check FMA first. Parts cannon stays in the toolbox.line mx gold
🎯

Flight Modes — Simplified Logic

LNAV and VNAV are FMC driven→LNAV follows the lateral route. VNAV follows the vertical profile.FMC driven
HDG SEL and V/S are pilot driven→HDG SEL follows MCP heading. V/S follows selected climb or descent rate.
LVL CHG is energy management→Pitch controls speed while A/T controls thrust.memory hook
⚙️

Trim Systems — Critical for MX

Speed trim works only when autopilot is OFF→It helps stability at low speed and high thrust.important condition
Mach trim works at high speed→It helps prevent nose-down tendency, also called mach tuck.
Only one FCC controls trim at a time→This avoids conflicting trim commands.no fighting
🛡️

Protection & Safety Logic

Autopilot will not engage unless conditions are correct→It uses interlocks for power, hydraulics, and system health.
If conditions change, autopilot drops offline→That can be normal behavior, not always a failed component.do not overcall
Actuators must sync before engagement→This prevents sudden control-surface movement.
🔧

Maintenance / Troubleshooting Gold

BITE is your #1 troubleshooting tool→Use CDU pages to find faults before guessing.best practice
Fault history is stored by flight leg→Great for intermittent autopilot or mode issues.
Always check sensor alignment and rigging marks→Bad inputs create bad autopilot behavior.input problem
Actuator issues need hydraulic and electrical checks→The actuator needs both to work correctly.
Autopilot problems are often sensor or mode logic issues→Do not assume the actuator is bad first.real mx reality
