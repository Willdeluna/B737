✅ B737 ATA 32 • Landing Gear & Brakes

Landing Gear & Brakes Made Line-Mechanic Simple

Hover over each idea. ATA 32 is not just gear and brakes — it's a system that manages energy, motion, and logic across multiple aircraft systems.

✈️
Absorb Energy
Shock struts and tires absorb landing impact through hydraulic damping and nitrogen spring.
🛑
Dissipate Energy
Brakes, antiskid, and autobrake convert kinetic energy into heat in a controlled way.
🛡️
Protect the System
Air/ground logic, transfer valves, and hydraulic fuses prevent damage when things go wrong.
🧠

Big Picture — Energy Management on the Ground

ATA 32 is all about handling huge loads safely→Absorb impact, control direction, dissipate energy.big picture
Cross-system dependency is the reality→Gear depends on hydraulics, electrical, and air/ground logic.system map
Most ATA 32 problems are not gear or brake hardware→They are usually sensors, valves, or logic signals.real mx reality
✈️

Shock Struts — Critical Understanding

Oleopneumatic = hydraulic + nitrogen→Fluid damps compression, nitrogen acts as the spring.design basics
Low fluid = hard landings / bottoming out→The strut can't absorb the impact properly.fault clue
Low nitrogen = poor rebound / squat issues→The strut sits low and doesn't return properly.fault clue
Strut servicing requires correct procedure→Wrong charge order leads to bad strut behavior.mx caution
🔄

Gear Operation & Sequencing

System A powers normal extension and retraction→Hydraulics drive the actuators and selector valves.primary power
Transfer valve provides backup→If System A is lost, System B can supply retraction pressure.redundancy
Door / gear timing is critical→Sequence prevents gear-to-door contact damage.sequence logic
Gear lever lock prevents ground retraction→Uses air/ground logic to block the lever.safety logic
⚠️

Air / Ground System — Crucial Concept

PSEU processes air/ground signals→Controls gear, brakes, pressurization, spoilers.central logic
Bad WOW signal = multiple unrelated write-ups→One bad sensor can cause faults across many systems.real mx reality
Proximity sensors do the actual sensing→Position determines air vs ground mode.sensor basics
Air/ground logic affects autobrake too→Touchdown protection prevents braking in flight.protection
🪶

Nose Wheel Steering

Two control inputs feed the steering→Tiller for high-angle taxi, rudder pedals for fine corrections.control inputs
Centering cams align nose wheel when airborne→Prevents damage on retraction into the wheel well.auto safety
Steering only works when strut compressed→WOW signal must be true for steering to operate.interlock
🛑

Brake System — Energy Dissipation

System auto-selects pressure source→Normal = System B, alternate = System A, emergency = accumulator.auto logic
Accumulator gives ~6 parking brake applications→Stored hydraulic pressure using nitrogen precharge.backup energy
Accumulator low = weak or no parking brake→Check nitrogen precharge during scheduled inspections.common issue
Hydraulic fuses isolate leaks automatically→Component loses pressure — looks like system failure but isn't.hidden cause
⚙️

Antiskid & Autobrake

Antiskid uses wheel speed transducers→Compares wheel behavior — releases pressure if skid detected.core function
Locked wheel protection prevents tire blowout→Stops brake pressure if a wheel locks up.safety
Autobrake uses deceleration rate, not pressure→Adjusts braking automatically to hit target rate.smart logic
AUTO BRAKE DISARM = pilot brake, thrust, or fault→Any of those will deactivate the autobrake system.normal disarm
🌡️

Brake Temperature

Heat is the biggest enemy of brakes→Hot brakes = fuse plug melt, fade, fire risk.real mx reality
Fuse plugs melt to deflate tires safely→Prevents tire explosion from heat buildup.safety design
Hot brakes delay maintenance and dispatch→Cooling required before further work.ops impact
🔧

Maintenance / Troubleshooting Gold

Manual extension needs hydraulic pressure removed→Otherwise actuators resist gravity drop.mx procedure
Brake issues = usually pressure source or sensor→Not the brakes themselves most of the time.real mx reality
Gear problems = sequence, sensors, or hydraulics→Mechanical gear failure is rare.best practice
Hydraulic fuses cause hidden system losses→A tripped fuse mimics a much bigger failure.trouble clue
Autobrake faults are usually logic issues→Sensor or signal problems, not autobrake hardware.input problem
