Doors & Windows Made Line-Mechanic Simple

Emergency Systems

Cargo & Access

Big Picture — Access, Sealing, Safety

Doors handle access AND pressurization integrity→Any door not latched = pressurization plus safety issue.big picture
Plug-type doors use cabin pressure to seal→Pressure pushes door into frame — latches don't carry load.key concept
Latches secure the door — pressure does the sealing→Latch issues = leak or warning, but structure still supported.design truth
🚪

Door Design — Plug Type

Door moves inward, then outward→Two-stage handle motion controls latches and gates.operation
Frame carries the pressure load, not the latches→Big change in mindset for new mechanics.design truth
Misrigged handle = poor seating→Leads to pressurization faults and warnings.real issue
⚠️

Door Warning System

Monitors latch position, not just "closed"→Proximity sensors and microswitches drive the warning.sensor logic
Processed by PSEU — same system as gear→Cross-system logic — gear and door faults can share root causes.cross-system
Door warning ≠ door open→Could just be a latch sensor issue.real mx reality
🪂

Entry Doors & Escape Slides

Armed slide WILL deploy when door opens→Most expensive mistake on the ramp.critical safety
Girt bar connects slide to floor brackets→Door opening pulls the inflation cable.mechanism
Slide inflates in ~6 seconds→Compressed gas plus aspirator does the work.operation
Always verify disarm before opening→Visual check the girt bar position.best practice
🔐

Flight Deck Door

Security door with electric + mechanical locking→Three control paths: cockpit switch, keypad, deadbolt.design
AUTO = normal locked-powered mode→UNLKD is temporary, DENY blocks even correct codes.modes
Decompression panels open at ~0.5 psi differential→Protects the door structure during rapid decompression.safety
🚨

Emergency Exits & Flight Lock

Overwing exits open inward then rotate ~125°→Plug-type design — same pressure logic as main doors.operation
Flight lock locks exits during flight→Triggered by engine running, air mode, thrust >53°.auto safety
Flight lock failure = master caution + lights→DOOR light and overwing light alert the crew.fault indication
Mid-cabin doors (-900ER) have solenoid lock→Prevents opening during takeoff roll.model specific
🪜

Airstairs

Forward airstair is electromechanical→AC + DC power with standby DC-only mode.design
Aft airstair is hydraulic→Electric pump or manual hand pump.design
Interlocks prevent in-flight deployment→Limit switches and relays do the safety work.safety
Common faults are limit switches and interlocks→Mechanical components rarely fail — sensors do.real mx reality
🚛

Main Deck Cargo Door

Hydraulically operated (System A)→Non-plug design — opens upward.design
Requires ground, hydraulics A, parking brake set→Multiple interlocks before door will operate.interlocks
Sequenced operation: latch → pull-in → lift→Motion is sequential, not simultaneous.key concept
UNLOCKED amber + thrust >53° = aural warning→Same logic as takeoff config warning.warning logic
📦

Lower Cargo Doors

Plug-type, inward opening→Counterbalance with spring and cable assists lifting.design
Uplock detent holds the door open→If counterbalance fails, door may drop suddenly.safety concern
🪟

Windows (ATA 56)

Flight deck windows are multi-layer laminated→Inner pane is structural — outer is scratch protection.design
Electrical heating provides anti-ice→Window heat controllers can fail and cause overheats.system
Passenger windows have 3 layers + breather hole→Outer is structural, middle is backup, inner is cosmetic.design
FO sliding window opens for emergency egress→Roller/track mechanism with latch system.feature
🔧

Maintenance / Troubleshooting Gold

Most door issues = sensors, rigging, switches→Structural problems are rare — signals are common.real mx reality
Always verify latching physically AND by indication→Don't trust the light alone — eyes on the latch.best practice
Slide deployment is the #1 ramp hazard→Check girt bar position before opening anything.critical safety
Cargo door = hydraulic + sequence issues→Watch the sequence — out of order = stuck door.diagnostic
Air/ground logic affects door locks→PSEU faults can lock or unlock doors incorrectly.cross-system
