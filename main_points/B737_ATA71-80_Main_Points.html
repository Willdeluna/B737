Big Picture — The Engine System

ATA 71-80 covers the engine + everything that runs it→Airflow, fuel, oil, indications, thrust reversers.big picture
Engine is electronically controlled by the EEC→Self-contained system — EEC is everything.core concept
Engine problems often show up in other systems first→Cross-system thinking is essential.real mx reality
✈️

Engine Core Design

CFM56-7 is a high-bypass turbofan→~80% thrust from fan (bypass), ~20% from core.design
Two shafts: N1 and N2→N1 = fan and LP turbine, N2 = compressor and HP turbine.rotation paths
Accessory Gear Box drives aircraft systems→Fuel pump, IDG, hydraulic pump, oil system.AGB role
⚙️

Airflow Path

Fan → LPC → HPC → Combustor → HPT → LPT→Standard turbofan flow path.flow map
N1 = airflow = big thrust→Fan speed is the primary thrust indicator.key concept
N2 = core power→Drives accessories and the compressor.key concept
🧠

EEC — Electronic Engine Control

EEC controls fuel, air, protection — everything→Dual channel (A + B) for redundancy.core function
Normal, Soft Alternate, Hard Alternate modes→Mode depends on data quality available to the EEC.modes
Hard Alternate = NO N1 overspeed protection→Critical to know during dispatch decisions.limitation
Auto-swaps channels on failure→Pilot rarely notices the swap happens.redundancy
🔥

Engine Start & Ignition

Bleed air spins starter at engine start→Same air source as ATA 36 — cross-system link.start logic
Fuel and ignition at ~7% N2→Starter cuts out around 55% N2.sequence
Hot start = high EGT — EEC cuts fuel→Protection prevents engine damage.fault clue
Igniters create ~15-20 kV spark→EEC decides when ignition is on.ignition system
⛽

Fuel System (Engine Side)

LP pump supplies, HP pump pressurizes→Two-stage fuel pump design.pump design
HMU controls fuel flow per EEC commands→Hydromechanical unit is the muscle.control element
HPSOV shuts off fuel for shutdown/fire→Fire handle activates HPSOV — primary shutdown.safety
No fuel = no engine→Most reliable way to shut the engine down.key concept
🛢️

Oil System

Three circuits: supply, scavenge, vent→Standard turbine oil system layout.design
Oil is fuel-cooled via heat exchanger→Crosses oil and fuel temperatures.cooling path
Low oil pressure = shutdown risk→Major write-up category for in-flight events.fault clue
Debris Monitoring System finds metal early→Tells you WHERE the failure is happening internally.diagnostic gold
🌡️

Engine Indicating

Primary: N1, EGT, fuel flow→Pilots watch these on every flight phase.primary display
Secondary: N2, oil, vibration→Secondary indications for health monitoring.secondary display
White/amber/red color logic→Tells you immediately if a parameter is in limit.display logic
8 thermocouples average EGT→EEC uses the averaged value for control.sensor design
AVM tracks vibration over 32 flights→High vibration = imbalance or internal damage.trend monitoring
🔄

Engine Air System

VSV controls compressor inlet air→Adjusts based on engine speed and conditions.compressor control
VBV dumps excess air to prevent stall→Critical during acceleration and transients.stall protection
TBV assists during start and acceleration→Smooths airflow during difficult operating points.transient aid
Clearance control adjusts turbine gaps→HPTACC and LPTACC keep efficiency high.efficiency
🔁

Thrust Reverser

Redirects fan air forward to slow the aircraft→Translating sleeve plus blocker doors.mechanism
Deploys only with proper air/ground signals→On ground and below 10 ft — interlock protected.safety logic
Auto-restow forces stowage if unsafe→Big safety feature — prevents in-flight deploy.protection
REV light = green = fully deployed→Amber = in transit. Fault light = system problem.indications
REVERSER faults = often sensor or sync lock→Mechanical sleeve failures are less common.real mx reality
🔧

Maintenance / Troubleshooting Gold

Most engine issues = sensors or EEC logic→Not mechanical failures most of the time.real mx reality
EEC is everything — check modes and data first→BITE pages tell you what the EEC sees.best practice
Hot starts and EGT exceedances are high priority→Always investigate — don't just reset.critical events
Many "engine" write-ups are ATA 36 problems→Bleed issues cascade into engine performance.cross-system
ENGINE CONTROL light = no dispatch→Serious fault that grounds the aircraft.dispatch impact
Follow: input (sensors) → EEC → output (fuel/air)→Logical diagnostic order for any engine fault.diagnostic flow
