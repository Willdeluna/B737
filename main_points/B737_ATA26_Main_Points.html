Detect, Alert, Extinguish

A quick-study Fire Protection page for line maintenance: what detects fire, what alerts the crew, what gets isolated, and what traps bite you later.

🔥
Detect
Fire loops, smoke detectors, overheat sensors, and pneumatic leak detection watch the airplane.
🚨
Alert
Master warning, master caution, bells, lights, handles, and BITE clues tell you where to look.
🧯
Extinguish
Engine, APU, cargo, and lav systems use bottles or automatic discharge to suppress fire.
🧠

Big Picture — What ATA 26 Does

System monitors fire, smoke, overheat, and pneumatic leaks→Different areas use different detection methods.
Two main functions: detect and extinguish→First find the problem, then isolate or suppress it.
Engine/APU use fire loops→Cargo/lav use smoke detection; wheel well and wing-body use overheat detection.
🔥

Engine Fire Detection

Each engine has two loops: A and B→Redundancy keeps one failed loop from taking the system down.redundancy
Normal mode uses AND logic→Both loops must agree to prevent false fire warnings.
If one loop fails, single-loop logic can be used→The remaining loop can still protect the engine.
Each engine has eight detectors→Four per loop spread around fan and core areas.
🌡️

How Detection Works

Detector is a gas-filled tube→Heat expands the gas, pressure rises, and switches trigger.
Three possible signals: overheat, fire, fault→Know which one you have before chasing parts.
Loss of pressure means loop fault, not fire→Huge troubleshooting point when the indication looks scary.MX trap
⚙️

Fire Detection Control Module

Control module reads loops and sends warnings→It is the logic brain between sensors and cockpit indications.
Overheat gives master caution→Important, but not the same as full fire warning.
Fire gives master warning, bell, and handle light→That is the full fire-warning package.
BITE lights help show failed area→Use BITE before throwing a loop or module at it.
⚠️

Fire Handles — Critical Operation

Pull handle = isolate the engine→Fuel, bleed air, hydraulics, generator, and thrust reverser are cut off or disabled.big clue
Fuel shutoff valve closes→Removes fuel source from the fire area.
Generator removed from system→Electrical load/source logic changes after handle pull.
Thrust reverser disabled→Prevents unsafe operation after engine isolation.
🧯

Engine Fire Extinguishing

Two bottles total, shared by both engines→Either bottle can be routed to either engine.
Handle turn selects bottle direction→CW fires one bottle, CCW fires the other.
Squib breaks the seal→Halon is released into the selected engine zone.
🧪

Bottle System Maintenance

Bottle pressure is about 800 psi at 70°F→Temperature matters when judging pressure.
Overheat can auto-vent the bottle→Safety relief protects the bottle from rupture.
DISCH light means used bottle or pressure lost→Do not assume it only means a commanded discharge.line clue
🔥

APU Fire System

APU has fire detection, no overheat loop→Do not troubleshoot it like the engine system.
Three sensing elements→They protect compartment and tailpipe areas.
APU fire causes automatic shutdown→Normal protective behavior, not a separate failure.
One extinguisher bottle only→No backup bottle for APU fire extinguishing.
🎛️

P8 Fire Protection Panel

P8 controls loop selection, tests, handles, and discharge→It is the cockpit command center for fire protection.
FIRE is red→Fire warning condition.
OVERHEAT and FAULT are amber→Differentiate caution/fault from actual fire warning.
📦

Cargo Fire System

Cargo smoke detection uses dual loops→A and B loops protect forward and aft compartments.
Smoke gives fire warning, bell, and lights→Cargo smoke is treated like a major fire event.
First bottle gives initial knockdown→About 60 minutes of suppression protection.
Second bottle meters release→Designed to keep fire suppressed long enough to land.
⚠️

Cargo System Trap

If aircraft lands before timer ends, reset CBs→If not reset, second bottle may discharge on next takeoff.classic test item
Timers can create surprise bottle discharge→Always check the cargo fire sequence after diversion or early landing.
🔥

Wheel Well / Wing Body Overheat

Wheel well detects fire or overheat→Uses thermistor-type sensing; resistance drops when hot.
CODC controls wheel well overheat detection→BITE stores faults and displays codes.
Wing/body overheat detects bleed air leaks→Think hot pneumatic air where it should not be.
Typical trips: 255°F and 310°F zones→Different areas have different temperature thresholds.
🚻

Lavatory Smoke & Fire

Each lav has its own smoke detector→Smoke triggers chime, lights, and master caution.
Lav extinguisher is fully automatic→No crew action needed once heat melts the plug.
Temperature strip changes color→Simple maintenance clue that heat/fire occurred.inspection clue
