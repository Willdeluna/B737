§1 What this covers

This document gives allowable damage limits for the section 43 wing-to-body fairing skin panels — the panels that fair over the join between fuselage section 43 and the wing root.

Limits only apply if the damage is sealed per Paragraph 2. Otherwise it becomes a repair.

What's allowed (Full Depth Core)

Cracks

  • Max one facesheet + core in depth
  • Size within Table 102 for the panel
  • Min distance from fasteners / edges
  • Min distance from other damage
  • Sealed per §2

Nicks / Gouges / Scratches

  • That don't damage glass fibers — permitted unconditionally
  • That do damage fibers — within Table 102 limits + min spacing + sealed

Dents

  • Within Table 102 dimension limit
  • Min distance from fasteners / edges / other damage
  • Sealed per §2

Holes / Punctures

  • Max one facesheet + core in depth
  • Within Table 102 dimension
  • Min spacing per Table 102
  • Sealed per §2

Delaminations

  • Within Table 102 dimension (use larger of visual or NDT-detected size)
  • Min spacing per Table 102
  • Sealed per §2

§4.C Formed Aluminum Sheet Panels

◇ Aluminum damage removal — typical taper geometry

Cracks · Nicks · Gouges · Scratches · Corrosion

  • Remove damaged material to 1.00" min radius
  • Depth X = max 0.10T (T = panel thickness)
  • Taper out at 20X minimum each direction
  • Distance from any edge / fastener / other damage = ≥ 20X
  • Smooth, no sharp creases

Dents

  • Depth Y = max 0.25" (6.4 mm)
  • W/Y ratio = 30 or more (a 1" deep dent needs 30" width)
  • Smooth, no sharp creases, no gouges, no cracks
  • No pulled or loose fasteners in the dent
  • Min 3.0D from other damage, holes, or panel edges

Holes / Punctures

  • Max 0.25" (6.4 mm) across largest dimension
  • Min 1.0" (25.4 mm) from fastener, edge, or other damage
  • Fill with 2017-T3 or 2117-T4 flush head rivet — no sealant

Corrosion around fasteners

  • Remove fasteners first, then the corroded material
  • Depth removal X = max 0.10T
  • 0.50" min radius around each affected fastener hole
  • Group limit: up to 3 adjacent fasteners in any group of 10
  • Install same-type fastener (up to first oversize) on re-assembly

Post-removal treatment (aluminum)

  1. Chemical conversion coating on all bare aluminum→ 51-20-01
  2. BMS 10-11 Type I primer, one layer→ SOPM 20-41-02
  3. Verify aerodynamic smoothness — non-compliance hits aircraft performance→ 51-10-01

§2 Sealing — GFRP Panels (no aluminum mesh)

Damage one ply deep or less

Temporary seal

interim
  1. Apply aluminum foil tape (speed tape)
  2. Record the location
  3. Check tape at normal maintenance intervals

Permanent seal

final
  1. BMS 8-201 / 8-207 / 8-301 epoxy resin to the area→ 51-70-08
  2. BMS 10-79 Type 3 or BMS 10-103 Type 1 primer, one layer→ SOPM 20-44-04
  3. BMS 10-60 enamel, one layer over the resin→ AMM 51-21-99/701

Damage more than one ply deep

  1. Use vacuum + heat to remove moisture from solid laminate or honeycomb cells→ 51-70-04
  2. Temporary seal with aluminum foil tape
  3. Record the location
  4. Repair within 24 months of the seal date — this is a hard limit

§2.A.3 Sealing — GFRP Panels WITH BMS 8-336 Aluminum Mesh

Different procedure Some panels have expanded aluminum foil mesh for lightning protection (visible on Figure 101). When the mesh itself is damaged, sealing follows a different reference.
  1. Damage limits for the mesh come from 51-70-14 — not this section
  2. Seal the damaged mesh area per 51-70-14

§2.B Sealing — Formed Aluminum Sheet

  1. Remove damaged material per 51-10-02
  2. Apply chemical conversion coating on bare surfaces→ 51-20-01
  3. Apply BMS 10-11 Type I primer, one layer→ SOPM 20-41-02
  4. Confirm aerodynamic smoothness — otherwise performance penalty→ 51-10-01
