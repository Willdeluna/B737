ATA 26 Tribal Knowledge

ATA 26 is NOT a fire system — it’s a detection + decision system. Sensors detect, modules decide, pilots act.

Core Rule: Most ATA 26 faults are NOT fires. Don't swap loops, bottles, or modules without checking dual-loop logic and contamination first.

OVHT but no FIRE. Replace detector loop?

❌ NOT YET. Check dual loop agreement and look for a local heat source.

Detector FAULT light. Replace engine?

❌ NO. Loop or wiring integrity issue — not an engine problem.

Cargo smoke detected. Replace smoke detector immediately?

❌ NO. Verify contamination vs real event first.

Bottle DISCH light. Replace bottle?

⚠️ CHECK PRESSURE FIRST. DISCH light triggers on low pressure too — don’t assume the bottle is empty.

Dual loop fire warning. Replace both loops?

❌ NO. Check control module and common wiring — dual false typically means upstream.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 26 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
Detection ≠ Fire — most alerts are NOT real fires.
Dual-loop logic is everything — NORMAL = AND, single loop = OR.
FAULT light = system integrity — not temperature.
Intermittent = wiring or vibration — not component failure.
Control module interprets everything — sensors aren't dumb, they report.
Fire handles isolate systems — fuel, hydraulics, bleed all shut off.
Extinguishing runs on hot battery — always available.
Cargo system is time-based survival — not instant extinguishing.
Contamination causes false alarms — especially cargo and lavatory.
Follow the path — Detector → Loop → Control Module → Cockpit → Squib.

🔚 Bottom Line

Detection systems use dual-loop AND logic. Fire handles isolate entire systems (fuel/hydraulics/bleed), not just fire fighting. Extinguishing runs on hot battery so it works even with a dead aircraft. Most alerts are contamination, wiring, or interpretation — not real fires.
