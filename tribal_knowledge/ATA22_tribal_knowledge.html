🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Speed Trim FAIL light illuminated. Should you change an FCC?

❌ NO. The light requires both FCCs to fail. One FCC failure alone may only show in BITE or recall.

Dual-channel autoland unavailable, no ILS faults. Change glideslope receiver?

❌ NO. Check DC bus isolation and autopilot engagement timing before touching ILS components.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 22 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
FCC power-up sequence matters — close AFCS/FCC DC breakers last.
Autopilot needs hydraulics — no pressure means no engagement.
Speed Trim stops when CMD engages — different trim systems.
LNAV/VNAV depend on data validity — FMC and ADIRU matter more than LRUs.
Some alerts need both FCCs failed — don’t chase ghosts.
Cam-out is not automatically actuator failure — it is protective logic.
Dual-channel autoland is strict — timing and bus isolation must be right.
Don’t erase fault history early — saved faults are gold.
Rigging errors mimic DFCS faults — flaps, stab, and elevators matter.
The FMA never lies — read it before troubleshooting.
