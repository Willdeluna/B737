Plug-type doors don’t rely on latches in flight — pressure holds them closed. But indication only cares about latch position and lock sensors.

Core Rule: DOOR CLOSED ≠ DOOR SAFE. Door latched + switches agree = SAFE. The PSEU is central to all door indications.

🎮 "Closed vs Latched" — Scenarios

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Door light on but door looks closed. Replace switch?

❌ NO. Latch rollers usually not fully seated.

Slide armed door opening. Open carefully?

❌ NO. ALWAYS confirm disarmed — deployment is instant.

Cargo door won’t open. Replace actuator?

❌ NO. Verify hyd A, parking brake, and gear down conditions.

Flight deck door won’t open. Mechanical jam?

❌ NO. Electric lock engaged by pilot — check switch position.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 52 is about alignment, not just movement. Most common mistake: 'door looks good' but the switch doesn’t agree. Latch rollers and sensor alignment cause more issues than electronics.
