🎮 "Heat or Logic Shutdown?" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Wing anti-ice OFF on takeoff. Failure?

❌ NO. Normal automatic switch-off for thrust conservation.

PITOT heat light ON. Probe overheating?

❌ NO. Light ON means no current flowing — it's a failure indication.

Window OVERHEAT won’t reset. WHCU bad?

❌ NO. Window must cool below threshold before reset works.

Poor rain visibility. Replace wipers?

❌ NO. Hydrophobic coating wears — check it first.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 30 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.

Probe heat = electric + current monitoring.

WTAI auto OFF on takeoff = normal.
Overheat protections exist mostly on ground.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 30 controls temperature intelligently. Air heats surfaces, electricity protects sensors and windows, and logic prevents damage. Many ground 'failures' are actually overheat protections.
