🎮 "Where Did the Pressure Go?" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

EDP LOW PRESS light. Replace EDP?

❌ NO. EDP OFF stops output, not rotation. Check switch and supply valve.

EMDP keeps tripping. Replace pump?

❌ NO. GFI is protecting against a fault. Find the fault first.

Both systems low pressure. Two pump failures?

❌ NO. Reservoir pressurization is shared — pneumatic supply could affect both.

PTU growling in flight. Malfunction?

❌ NO. Normal when System B EDP fails or flaps not up. It's working.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 29 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
Hydraulics = pressure — not flow confusion.
EDP OFF stops output, NOT rotation.
Fire switch kills hydraulic supply instantly.
EMDP failures are often electrical protection — not hyd.
Reservoir pressurization is critical — from pneumatics.
PTU = backup ONLY for System B high lift.
Standby system is a logic-triggered safety net.
Filters cause more problems than pumps sometimes.
LOW PRESS lights are threshold-based — not always failures.
Always ask: where did the pressure stop?

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 29 is a pressure management system. Pumps create it, valves route it, and logic protects it. System A handles gear/steering/alt brakes; System B handles flaps/brakes; Standby is the safety net.
