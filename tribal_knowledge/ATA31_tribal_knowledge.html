🎮 "Bad Data or Bad Display?" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Display blank. Replace DU?

❌ NO. DU may auto-recover from overheat. Check power first.

All displays show same thing. CDS failed?

❌ NO. ALL ON 1 / ALL ON 2 selector forces one DEU.

PFD shows SPD flag. Replace display?

❌ NO. Flag indicates source system failure — not display.

Master caution on. Replace MC module?

❌ NO. MC is generic — find the system annunciator that’s lit.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 31 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
CDS is a display system, not a source system.
Ask: is the data wrong, or just displayed wrong?
DEUs = brains of the display system.
Displays auto reconfigure — not always a failure.
CDS FAULT = big problem — DEU, config, or power.
Master caution = attention, not diagnosis.
System annunciator = actual fault location.

BITE is your best friend for quick isolation.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 31 is all about getting information to the crew correctly. If info is missing, trace upstream. If wrong, check the source system. If blank, check power/display. DEUs are the brains — displays just show what they're told.
