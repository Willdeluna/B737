🎮 "Protection or Failure?" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

BLEED TRIP OFF. Reset immediately?

❌ NO. Wait for temperature/pressure to return to normal first.

Low duct pressure. Replace PRSOV?

❌ NO. BAR controls the PRSOV — check the command first.

Repeated bleed trips. Replace temp sensor?

❌ NO. Dirty/clogged precooler is the usual cause.

Wing-body overheat. Replace sensors?

❌ NO. Usually an actual duct leak — sensors are doing their job.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 36 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
PRSOV is controlled — not the problem.
BAR is the brain of bleed control.
Most shutdowns = protection, not failure.
High temp → system REDUCES flow.
Isolation valve logic = switch-based.
APU bleed needs BOTH signal AND pressure.
Precooler cleanliness is critical.
WTAI overrides cooling logic (ground).
Bleed trip requires reset AFTER normalization.
Separate: control problem vs airflow problem.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 36 is a protection system, not just air plumbing. If something shuts down, the airplane is saying 'something was about to overtemp or overpressure.' Always separate control problem from airflow problem.
