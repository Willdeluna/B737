🎮 "Fuel That Won't Move" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Surge tank discharge. Tank leak?

❌ NO. Surge tanks collect overflow — look at the vent system.

Center tank pump auto shuts off. Replace pump?

❌ NO. 15-second auto shutoff is normal protection.

Engine won’t get fuel. Replace boost pumps first?

❌ NO. Spar valve has final authority. Check it before pumps.

Fueling valve won’t open. Replace valve?

❌ NO. Check switch position, float switch, and fuel pressure first.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 28 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
Fuel problems are often logic problems — not leaks.
Boost pump LP light ≠ always a failure — it triggers on switch OFF too.
Center tank pumps auto shutoff by design.
Crossfeed must be manually opened — fuel doesn’t transfer by gravity.
Spar valve = final authority to engine.
APU can run without boost pumps — suction feed.
Float switches end fueling — not gauges.
IMBAL and CONFIG are operational — not hardware faults.
Vent system problems show as leaks or relief valves.
GFI/UFI protection = fire prevention — not nuisance.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 28 is about control of fuel, not just storage. Fuel must be stored safely, moved correctly, and protected from ignition. Pumps, valves, and float switches do most of the work — logic decides what's allowed.
