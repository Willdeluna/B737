🎮 "Is It Logic or Mechanical?" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Gear won’t retract. Replace selector valve?

❌ NO. Lever lock requires air signal. Check air/ground logic first.

PSEU light on. Replace PSEU?

❌ NO. PSEU is reporting a condition — read its memory first.

AUTOBRAKE DISARM. Real failure?

⚠️ MAYBE. Many conditions auto-disarm — understand why before replacing.

No brakes. Replace brake unit?

❌ NO. Check all 3 pressure sources (B, A, accumulator).
