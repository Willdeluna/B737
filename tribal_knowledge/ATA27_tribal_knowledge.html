🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

Flight control feels heavy. Replace surface first?

❌ NO. Feel force is artificial. Check hydraulic vs feel vs mechanical.

Flap stops mid travel. Force movement?

❌ NO. Bypass valve has locked the system out. Investigate the protection trip.

Spoilers not working. Replace actuator?

❌ NO. Check interlock and shutoff valves first.

Trim issue — jump to actuator?

❌ NO. Check electrical logic and cutout switches first.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 27 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
Separate mechanical / hydraulic / electrical — they're three different problems.
Dual systems mean partial control still exists — A/B hydraulic logic.
Feel ≠ actual control problem — it’s often artificial.
Spoilers have NO manual backup — hydraulic or nothing.
Most flap faults are PROTECTION doing its job.
Speedbrake logic depends on many inputs — not just the lever.
Trim systems have multiple safeties — learn them before troubleshooting.
Rudder is intentionally limited at speed — above ~137 kt.
Many “failures” are normal automatic functions.
If system stops itself, it detected something unsafe.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 27 isn't just controls — it's protection-driven control. Pilot commands → system modifies → protections override. Separate mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical layers and dual-system logic means partial control usually still exists.
