🎮 "PACKs Broke the Jet" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

PACK trips once on climb. No repeat. Change the pack?

❌ NO. Single PACK trips are often transient airflow or thermal events. Investigate first.

Weak cabin cooling reported only on hot days. Change ACM?

❌ NO. Check water spray, heat exchangers, and ram airflow first. High-OAT failures usually trace to poor cooling efficiency, not ACM failure.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 21 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
PACK trips are usually airflow problems — not bad packs.
Heat exchangers get dirty faster than you think — inspect them early.
APU HIGH flow requires multiple conditions — not just the switch.
The coldest zone controls pack output — averaging does not exist.
ZONE TEMP lights often latch — they don't self-clear.
Trim air OFF is a powerful isolation step — use it early.
RAM DOOR FULL OPEN is diagnostic, not normal — system is compensating.
AUTO pressurization failure ≠ loss of pressurization — controller swap first.
Equipment cooling logic changes with smoke or ground conditions — don't troubleshoot blindly.
If airflow is wrong, everything downstream lies — fix flow before chasing faults.
