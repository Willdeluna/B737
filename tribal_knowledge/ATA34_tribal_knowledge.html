ATA 34 Tribal Knowledge

ATA 34 is not about sensors — it’s about TRUSTED DATA. Failures are rarely hardware alone: sensor mismatch, bad data path, or alignment.

Core Rule: Always ask 'who disagrees with who?' AND 'what is fooling me?' Standby agreeing with crew may mean standby is the only correct source.

🎮 "Find the Lie" — Scenarios

Read first. Guess. Then reveal.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

ALIGN won’t complete. Replace IRU?

❌ NO. Often just needs position entered twice or aircraft to stop moving.

Multiple NAV symptoms. Replace ADIRU?

❌ NO. Pitot/static feeds many systems — check ports first.

Standby disagrees with primaries. Replace standby?

❌ NO. Standby is often the only correct source.

Autoland INOP, everything else OK. Replace autopilot?

❌ NO. Radio altimeter mismatch is the usual cause.

🛠 Top 10 ATA 34 Tribal Knowledge Rules

The stuff that saves time and parts.
ADIRS failure = split problem — ADR vs IR.
Pitot/static = root cause of many systems.
GPS issues often = antenna or ADIRU input.
RA is critical — for autoland and GPWS.
ILS failures = usually signal, not hardware.
ISFD = last reliable reference.
Alignment problems = position or movement.
ARINC 429 issues = hidden killers.
Always check BITE before replacing parts.
Ask: is the data wrong, missing, or disagreed?

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 34 troubleshooting is NOT about replacing boxes. It's about data validity, source comparison, and finding the lie. Pitot/static feeds everything — a tiny contamination can cause many symptoms.
