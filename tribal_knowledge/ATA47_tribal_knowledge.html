ATA 47 Tribal Knowledge

ATA 47 is a safety system (fuel tank inerting), not performance. It can be degraded and still fly. Shuts down quietly when conditions aren't right.

Core Rule: Most 'failures' are the system protecting itself or not allowed to run. Chase conditions, cooling, and contamination — not components.

🎯 Change the LRU or Pass?

Answer YES or NO before revealing why.

NGS not running on ground. Failed?

❌ NO. Ground = normally inhibited.

ASM poor output. Replace ASM?

❌ NO. Upstream contamination typically causes ASM degradation.

BLUE light. Repair required immediately?

❌ NO. Degraded but operational — trackable defer.

Overtemp shutdown. Replace controller?

❌ NO. Cooling side problem — ram air valve or heat exchanger.

🔚 Bottom Line

ATA 47 doesn't fail loudly — it silently protects the aircraft. The biggest mistake is chasing components instead of conditions, cooling, and contamination.
